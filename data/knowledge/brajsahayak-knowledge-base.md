# BrajSahayak Knowledge Base — Temples, Parking, Routes & Nearby Places (Mathura–Vrindavan–Braj Region)

This document is the reference knowledge source for the BrajSahayak AI assistant, ingested into the
Amazon Bedrock Knowledge Base from S3. It covers 32 temples and sacred sites across Mathura, Vrindavan,
Govardhan, Radha Kund, Barsana, Nandgaon, Gokul, and Baldeo — with general timings, the route to reach
the temple, suggested/nearby parking, last-mile travel guidance, and nearby food, shopping, and stays.

## How the assistant should use this data

- All timings, parking spots, routes, and prices in this document are **reference data**, not live data.
  Always tell the visitor to verify locally before travelling.
- Never promise parking availability. Parking is "suggested" or "nearby," not guaranteed or reserved.
- Prefer the specific parking references and routes for a temple's own entry over generic advice.

## Zone-based parking and routing rules

Apply these general rules whenever a temple-specific entry does not already cover the situation:

- **Old Mathura or old Vrindavan temples**: Recommend outer parking. Recommend walking or e-rickshaw for
  the final stretch. Warn that private vehicles may not enter the narrow lanes.
- **Chhatikara Road temples**: Recommend parking near Prem Mandir, Maa Vaishno Devi Temple, Rukmani
  Vihar, or another listed outer parking. Recommend walking or e-rickshaw.
- **Barsana, Nandgaon, Govardhan, Gokul, or Baldeo temples**: Recommend town parking or designated
  festival parking. Warn that hilltop temples may require walking or stairs.
- **During festivals** (any temple, any zone):
  - Set aside the normal route and parking assumptions above.
  - Show this notice instead: "Check current traffic diversion before travel."
  - Never promise parking availability.

## Festival / uncertainty disclaimer

Show this notice whenever a festival, high-crowd date, or otherwise uncertain situation is detected,
or whenever the assistant is not confident current conditions match this reference data:

> Important: Temple timings, parking capacity, charges, entry rules, route diversions, and nearby
> business availability may change because of festivals, weather, security arrangements, and local
> administration. This information is reference data only. Please verify locally before travelling.
